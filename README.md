# Relic Card

The Relic Card exam, the arena, and now a game.

## Play THE FOREST BETWEEN

**[Play it here](https://p13ces.github.io/Relic-card/forest-between-rpg.html)**

A Game Boy style story RPG set in the world of Relic Age: New Horizon. Act One is playable start to finish, right in the browser. No install.

Take the licensing exam and get issued your own Relic Card. Walk the forest road to Bishop Shelley's chapel. Then hold the old cut at first light.

### What's inside

- **The exam.** Name your traveler, answer the five stations, and get your card. Your answers set your PA / SA / MA affinities.
- **Act One, from the prose.** Shelley, Arch Saint Moffat, the meat, the tied-shut pouch, and the six statues. "There were seven. Now there are six."
- **The Toll.** When the bell rings, everything on the map freezes. Stand still.
- **Arena combat on canon rules.** 8x8 grid, 2 AP per activation, AN costs, Strain bands (Safe / Amber / Critical), Borrowed Time counters, Dad's Candle as your free relic, and the 6-Tick Escalation Clock.

### Controls

- **Walk:** arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen D-pad
- **A:** talk and confirm (Z, Space, or Enter)
- **B:** back, and view your Relic Card (X)
